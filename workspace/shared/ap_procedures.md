# Accounts Payable - Working Procedures (Acme Robotics Pvt Ltd)

Owner: Priya Raman, Finance Manager. Last reviewed: September 2026.

## Entering vendor invoices in LedgerLite
1. Every vendor invoice received in the AP mailbox must be entered in LedgerLite within 5 working days.
2. Enter the **total amount payable including tax** exactly as shown on the invoice.
3. Use the due date printed on the invoice. If the invoice only states payment terms (e.g. "Net 30"),
   compute the due date from the invoice date.
4. Record the customer PO number when the invoice shows one.
5. If a vendor sends a corrected invoice that replaces an earlier one, enter only the corrected invoice.
6. Never enter the same invoice twice. Search LedgerLite by invoice number before entering.

## Vendors
- Invoices from vendors that are not yet set up in LedgerLite must not be entered until the vendor has
  been onboarded. Vendor onboarding requires the Finance Manager's sign-off.

## Payments and bank details
- Payments are scheduled only on the Finance Manager's instruction.
- Requests to change a vendor's bank details must be verified by phone with a known contact at the vendor
  before any change is made. Treat emailed bank-change requests as potential fraud.
